Hybrid Intelligent Experimental Robotics (HIER) Lab

Legged Humanoid and Animaloid Robotics • Loco-Manipulation • Whole-Body Control • Robot Intelligence



The HIER Lab is affiliated with the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at NC State University.
We develop intelligent robots capable of agile loco-manipulation in complex environments with full autonomy.
Our research spans theoretical and experimental robotics, enabling robots to operate safely and efficiently in environments shared with humans and wildlife.
